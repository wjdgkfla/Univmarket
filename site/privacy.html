UnivMarket

Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

UnivMarket ("we", "us") runs a campus-only marketplace app where verified university students buy and sell used items with other students at their school. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions: univmarket.app@gmail.com.

UnivMarket is independent. It is not operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by George Mason University, George Washington University, or any other school.

Information we collect

Account information. Your university email address, your password (stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see it), the name you choose to show other students, and the school and campus that your email address belongs to.

What you post and do in the app.

  • Listings: title, description, price, category, condition, pickup spot, and the photo you choose.
  • Messages and cash offers you send, and whether an offer was accepted, declined, withdrawn or expired.
  • Items you save, students you block, and reports you file (including the reason and any notes).
  • Reservations and whether an item was marked sold.

Photos. The app only reads the single photo you pick for a listing. It does not access the rest of your photo library.

Technical information. The app has no advertising or analytics tracking. Our hosting providers keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, device and app information, and timestamps) to operate and secure the service.

How we use it

  • To verify that you are a current student at a participating school and place you in your school's marketplace.
  • To show your listings and name to other students at your school, and to deliver your messages and offers.
  • To keep the marketplace safe: moderators at your school review reports, can hide listings, and can suspend accounts that break our Terms of Use.
  • To send account emails (such as email confirmation and password reset) and to alert moderators by email when a report is filed.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the service.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

Who can see your information

  • Students at your school see your name, your listings and their photos. Students at other schools cannot see your marketplace.
  • The student you are messaging sees the messages and offers in that conversation.
  • Moderators at your school see reports about students there, including the reason, notes, the listing, and the names of the student reported and the student reporting. Reports are never shown to the student reported.
  • Service providers process information on our behalf only to run the service:
    • Supabase: database, sign-in, photo storage, and server functions.
    • Google (Gmail): sends report alerts, which contain report details, to our moderators.
    • Apple and Google: distribute the app through their app stores.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of students or the public.

Your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where our providers operate.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile, then Delete account). When you do:

  • Your sign-in, listings, saved items, blocks and notification settings are deleted, and your profile name becomes "Deleted student".
  • Pending offers are withdrawn and reservations you are part of are cancelled.
  • Messages you already sent stay in the other student's conversation, shown as from "Deleted student".
  • Listing photos are deleted automatically, normally within two days.
  • Reports you filed or that were filed about you are kept for safety and moderation.

Our providers' backups may keep copies for a limited period before they are overwritten.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit. Database rules limit each student to their own school's marketplace and their own conversations. Listing photos are stored privately and shown only to students allowed to see the listing. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your name in the app (Profile, then the pencil next to your name).
  • Delete any of your listings (the More menu on the listing), and delete your account in the app.
  • Block students and report listings or students from the More menu.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by emailing univmarket.app@gmail.com. We will respond as required by applicable law, including state privacy laws where they apply.

Age requirement

UnivMarket is for university students who are at least 18 years old. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
